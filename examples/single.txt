The kingdom has $$$n$$$ cities connected by $$$m$$$ bidirectional roads, and the $$$i$$$-th road takes $$$w_i$$$ minutes to travel. A courier starts in city $$$1$$$ and must deliver a parcel to city $$$n$$$. Exactly $$$k$$$ cities contain a checkpoint, and the courier has to pass through at least one of them on the way. Find the minimum time needed to complete the delivery, or print $$$-1$$$ if city $$$n$$$ cannot be reached through a checkpoint.
